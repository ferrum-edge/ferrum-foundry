### Limits and pending work

Root must obtain a fresh independent read-only review and qualify every required
job for the final source and tested merge of PR #548: Qualification Source,
Quality Gate on Node 22 and 24, Pinned Gateway Contract (including writable/read-only
parity), Deployment Starter, Critical
Journeys and both Container Gates. The producer's Gateway Contract job passed
at its earlier source, but both Node Quality Gates failed an old readiness
fixture. Its repair and this provenance update need fresh gates. Only a
qualification-evidence change may follow a qualified source without restarting
qualification. Publication is a separate root-owned step after those gates.

This pin adoption adds no conditional restore, native deployment workflow,
recovery journal, capability or browser retention guarantee. Existing initial
untagged-write fallback and post-412 refusal remain unchanged; Foundry #542/#544
owner approval and #547 account-level immutability decisions are pending.
The deployment-snapshot refusal does not authorize browser retention of its
original evidence.

No advisory closure or unrelated upstream approval is implied. Edge #6011 is
excluded from v0.9.12. Existing best-effort/unqualified modes, eBPF variants,
real-gateway scale limits and browser limits remain unchanged.
